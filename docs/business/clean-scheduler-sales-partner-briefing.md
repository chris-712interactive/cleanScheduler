## The simple idea

Cleaning companies quote the job, put crews on the calendar, send the invoice, and let customers pay from the same place. That is Clean Scheduler. It already works. Your job is to get it in front of cleaning companies and help them say yes.

You get paid for the companies you bring in and close. Chris builds the product, sets each company up, and helps after they buy. You both sell. A shared board shows who gets credit.

This is extra income on your own time. We put the pay terms in writing before the first demo. As you build a book of companies, we can talk about a longer setup.

## How you get paid

When a company you brought in pays:

1. You get **25%** of the money they pay in year one. On Business yearly that is **$309** the day they pay.
2. You also get **$12.90 each month** for **24 months** while they stay. That is **$154.80** in year one and **$154.80** in year two. **One yearly company = about $464 in year one, about $619 over two years if they stay.**
3. The name on the lead in the sales board is who gets paid.

If a brand-new company cancels in the first 90 days, we adjust that commission so it stays fair.

### If they pay monthly instead

Yearly is the close we want. Monthly still counts. You get paid when they pay us — not on a promise.

On Business monthly (**$129** each month):

1. **25% of year-one payments** as Stripe collects them: **$32.25 each month** for 12 months. If they stay the year that is **$387**. If they cancel sooner, you only get 25% of what they actually paid.
2. **Residual is the same on monthly or yearly:** **$12.90 each month for 24 months** while they stay. That is 10% of the **$129 list price**, not of the discounted yearly price.

|                           | Yearly (aim here)                  | Monthly                                |
| ------------------------- | ---------------------------------- | -------------------------------------- |
| They pay                  | $1,236 the day they buy            | $129 each month                        |
| Your 25%                  | $309 that day                      | $32.25 each month in year one          |
| Residual                  | $12.90 / month for 24 months       | Same                                   |
| Year one if they stay     | About **$464**                     | About **$542**                         |
| Two years if they stay    | About **$619**                     | About **$697**                         |
| If they cancel in month 6 | $309 + 6 × $12.90 = about **$386** | 6 × ($32.25 + $12.90) = about **$271** |

Monthly can pay you a little more over two years because they did not get the yearly discount. You still sell yearly: cash in the door on day one, and they are more likely to stay. Same 25% + 10% of list price on Starter or Pro if that is what they buy.
